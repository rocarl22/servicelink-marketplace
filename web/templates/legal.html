Terms & privacy

Draft summary - have a qualified lawyer review before public launch

What we collect

Your name, phone number, optional email, area, profile text, requests, messages, jobs and reviews, plus basic security logs (sign-in times, IP address and browser type) to protect accounts.

How we use it

To run the marketplace: matching you with providers or clients, showing job progress, keeping chat safe, and investigating abuse. Phone numbers and emails are not shown to other users.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, sign out of other devices, and download your data from Settings. To close your account, contact the administrator.

Acceptable use

Be honest, be respectful, don't try to take deals off the platform, and don't share other people's information.

Responsibility

ServiceLink connects people; each job is an agreement between the client and the provider.
