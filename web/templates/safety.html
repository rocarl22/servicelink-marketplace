Staying safe on ServiceLink

A few simple habits protect you and the people you work with.

{{ icon('lock') }} Keep it on the platform

Phone numbers, emails and links are hidden in chat on purpose. Requests to pay outside the app or move to another messenger are a common scam sign.

{{ icon('shield-check') }} Before the job

Check the provider's reviews and completed jobs, agree the price in the job page, and confirm what's included.

{{ icon('circle-check') }} After the job

Only confirm the work once you're satisfied, then leave an honest review. Never pay anyone who pressures you to skip confirmation.

{{ icon('triangle-alert') }} If something feels wrong

Stop replying, don't share personal or banking details, and contact the ServiceLink administrator. Never share your password with anyone - ServiceLink staff will never ask for it.
